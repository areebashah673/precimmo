What is Precision Immuno-Oncology

Precision immuno-oncology is a specialized cancer treatment that uses the immune system to target and destroy cancer cells with high specificity. It employs advanced methods to analyze a patient’s genetics, immune profile, and tumor biology, enabling personalized therapy..
